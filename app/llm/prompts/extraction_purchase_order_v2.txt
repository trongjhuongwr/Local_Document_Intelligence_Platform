Extract the purchase order fields from the document above into JSON.

Field guide:
- po_number: copy the value after "PO Number:", e.g. "PO-2025-1234".
- vendor_name: the company under the "Vendor" heading.
- customer_name: the company under the "Bill To" heading.
- issue_date: from the "Issue Date:" line, YYYY-MM-DD.
- currency: the 3-letter code (USD, EUR or GBP).
- approved_amount: copy the value after "Approved Amount:" exactly, e.g. "EUR 14,600.00".
- Write "null" for any value the document does not state. Never invent values.
